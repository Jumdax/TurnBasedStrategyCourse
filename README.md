# HOLLOWDEEP

HOLLOWDEEP is a turn-based tactical dungeon crawler.

Guide your Fighter and Priest through the dungeon, survive its enemies, and find the final treasure chest to escape.

You do not need to explore every room or defeat every enemy.

## Objective

Find and open the final treasure chest to escape HOLLOWDEEP.

- Explore the dungeon.
- Fight or avoid enemies.
- Use treasure chests to recover health.
- Keep at least one hero alive.
- Open the final treasure chest to win.

If all of your heroes fall, the game is over.

## Heroes

### Fighter

The Fighter is your primary melee combatant.

Actions:

- **Move** — Move to another reachable location.
- **Melee** — Attack an adjacent enemy.
- **Open Chest** — Open a nearby treasure chest.

### Priest

The Priest can fight in melee combat and use Smite against enemies.

Actions:

- **Move** — Move to another reachable location.
- **Melee** — Attack an adjacent enemy.
- **Smite** — Strike an enemy with a magical attack.
- **Open Chest** — Open a nearby treasure chest.

## Controls

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| WASD | Move camera |
| Q / E | Rotate camera |
| Mouse Wheel | Zoom |
| Left Click | Select / Act |
| Fighter / Priest Buttons | Select hero |
| END TURN | End your current turn |
| EXIT TO TITLE | Abandon the current run and return to the title screen |

## Playing the Game

1. Adjust the volume on the title screen if desired.
2. Select **START**.
3. Select the Fighter or Priest using the hero buttons or by clicking the hero.
4. Select an available action.
5. Choose a valid target or destination.
6. Use **END TURN** when you are finished with your heroes' actions.
7. Survive the enemy turn and continue exploring.
8. Find and open the final treasure chest to escape.

## Treasure Chests

Opening a treasure chest restores all currently living heroes to full health.

Defeated heroes are not resurrected.

The final treasure chest also heals surviving heroes before completing the game.

## Victory

Open the final treasure chest.

**YOU ESCAPED HOLLOWDEEP**

## Defeat

If all player heroes are defeated:

**THE PARTY HAS FALLEN**

## Tips

- You do not have to defeat every enemy.
- You do not have to explore every room.
- Chests can restore your surviving heroes to full health.
- Use the Fighter and Priest together.
- Sometimes avoiding a fight is better than taking unnecessary damage.

Good luck escaping HOLLOWDEEP.
